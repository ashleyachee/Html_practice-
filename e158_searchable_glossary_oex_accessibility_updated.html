A
actionable

a presentation or finding that ends on a specific next step the audience can take, rather than ending on the finding alone

aggregation

the way individual records are combined into a single value (e.g., sum, average, count) before being charted

aggregation choice

the decision of which arithmetic (sum, average, count) combines individual records into the value a chart displays

analytics

the practice of examining data to find patterns and support a decision

association

a claim that names a pattern between two measures without asserting that one causes the other

assumption

a decision made during data preparation or analysis that affects the result, such as how a late delivery is defined or whether a canceled order is counted

audience fit

whether a message’s detail level, language, visual emphasis, and action all match what a specific audience needs

audience need

the specific thing a particular stakeholder must know before they can act, which differs depending on their role

B
bar chart

a chart that shows one separate bar per discrete category, sized to that category's value, for comparing categories at a single point in time rather than a trend over time

bin size

the width of the value ranges a histogram groups a measure into; a choice that changes the chart's apparent shape without changing the underlying data

box plot

a chart that summarizes a measure's spread (median, quartiles, range) for one or more groups at once

business intelligence (BI)

a decision-support process that uses data to help someone act, distinct from reporting or dashboarding on their own

business question

the specific question a decision maker needs answered before they can act

C
calculated field

a new column built by computing a value from other fields in the same table, such as a Days Late column computed as Actual Delivery Date minus Promised Date

chart-question alignment

whether a finished chart's type, fields, and aggregation match the business question it was built to answer

claim

the conclusion being defended, stated plainly, as the first of four parts in a complete defense

coherence

whether a finished dashboard's purpose, worksheet selection, layout, and title work together to answer its intended question without outside explanation

comparison

a question asking how one category or group's measure stacks up against another's

composition

a question asking how a total breaks into its component parts

confounding factor

a third variable connected to both measures in a correlation, capable of producing the pattern without either measure directly affecting the other

consistency

using the same label and the same color meaning for a measure across every worksheet on a dashboard

context

the situation that prompted a dashboard or finding to be built in the first place

continuous

a smooth numeric or date range, shown in green in Tableau, such as Qty Ordered or Order Date treated as a timeline

correlation

a relationship between two measures described by direction, form, and strength, without claiming that one causes the other

cost

the routine price paid to fulfill an order, unit cost of goods plus standard freight, before anything is rushed

current state

the situation as it exists right now, including the problem a DMAIC project is meant to address

D
dashboard

a combined view of multiple worksheets, arranged so they may answer one or more business questions that are part of the overall problem statement, and help with monitoring, measuring, and analyzing relevant data in key areas

data cleaning

the general process BI professionals use to prepare any dataset to use, regardless of the tool

data pane

the panel in Tableau's workspace where every field from a connected sheet appears, already sorted into dimensions and measures

data profiling

the practice of examining a dataset to uncover what is normal, what is missing, and what is going wrong in it

data quality

whether a dataset is accurate, complete, and consistent enough to support the decision you're about to make with it

data story

a one-time, guided explanation built to walk a specific audience through a specific conclusion, as distinct from a dashboard built for repeated reference

date granularity

the level of detail a date field is grouped by (day, week, month, quarter, year), which changes a chart's shape without changing the underlying data

decision-support

an approach to using data and analysis that is aimed at guiding an upcoming decision, not just describing what already happened

defects

an output that fails to meet a requirement, such as a shipment that arrives short or damaged

defensible BI data story

a presentation that is evidence-tied, adapted to its audience, presented with its tradeoffs named, ready to explain its methods in plain language, and ready to answer a live question without overclaiming

Delivery Days

a calculated field equal to Delivery Date minus Order Date

delivery variance

the gap between the date a supplier promised a shipment and the date it arrived

demand

the volume to be fulfilled each month, and the baseline against which every other KPI category is read

description

a claim that states only what is directly visible in a chart, with no interpretation added

design rationale

a short explanation connecting a dashboard's specific design choices back to the purpose it was built to serve

discrete

a distinct, separate value, shown in blue in Tableau, such as Category or Supplier

distribution

a question asking how spread out a single measure is across many records

distribution center

a facility a company uses to receive, store, and ship inventory to retailers or customers

DMAIC

a five-phase process, define, measure, analyze, improve, control, used to guide an improvement effort from problem to solution

documentation

a stated definition of a dashboard's key terms, date range, and thresholds, so viewers don't have to assume them

drill-down

an interactive feature that moves a viewer from a summary view to more detail within the same dashboard

duplicate records

the same event entered twice

E
expedited freight fees

extra charges paid to rush a shipment when a supplier or carrier is running behind

explanatory visualization

a chart built to communicate a specific, already identified finding to an audience

exploratory visualization

a chart built to look for a pattern in data before deciding what, if anything, to conclude from it

F
filter

a mechanism that narrows a chart or view to only the data relevant to the specific question being asked

filtering

leaving out rows that don't belong in a particular analysis, such as a canceled order or a test entry

forecast

a claim about data that does not exist yet; distinct from a trend description, which only characterizes data that has already been observed

format check

confirming that a column is stored as the right data type, such as making sure a date column is stored as a date and not text

G
governance

control over who can access, see, or change a shared view of data

H
hallucination

a case where an AI model produces a plausible-sounding but false or unverifiable answer

heat map

a chart that uses color across two dimensions to flag exceptions or status at a glance

hierarchy

the order in which a dashboard layout's size and position direct a viewer's attention across a dashboard's worksheets

highlight table

a grid of values with color indicating status

histogram

a chart that groups a single measure into ranges (bins) to show how its values are spread out

horizontal container

a Tableau layout element that groups worksheets side by side

hovering

resting your mouse pointer directly over a chart mark, without clicking, to bring up a tooltip's value

I
implication

what a finding means for operations if it continues unaddressed

improved state

the situation a DMAIC project is aiming for once the goal statement is achieved

inconsistent categories

the same thing spelled, capitalized, or typed differently across rows

index

a way of rescaling two or more series to a common starting value, such as 100, so they can be compared on the same axis regardless of their original units

insight

a conclusion drawn from a chart or pattern in data, distinct from the data or chart itself

insight statement

a concise, evidence-based sentence that names a specific value, pattern, or exception from a dataset

intellectual property

data or work a person or organization owns, including how it can be used or shared**

interactive dashboard

a dashboard where the viewer can adjust filters, parameters, or variables to change what is displayed

invalid dates

a date that can't exist, like month 13

inventory

the quantity of materials, work in process, finished goods, and supplies physically on hand at a single point in time, unlike demand, which is measured over a period of time

J
join

combining two tables into one by matching a shared field between them, such as matching Supplier names between a company’s Orders data and its Supplier reference table

K
KPI

a measurable value used to track how well a process or goal is performing

KPI-style view

a chart, often a color-coded table, built to show whether a set of measures is meeting a threshold rather than to show shape or trend

L
Lean Six Sigma

a process-improvement framework used to classify costs like waste and to structure improvement efforts through phases such as DMAIC

line chart

a chart that connects each point in a continuous sequence, such as month over month, into a single trend, rather than treating each value as its own separate bar

M
matching key

the field used to join two tables together, which must match exactly between them for the join to work correctly

method

a brief, plain-language account of how the evidence behind a claim was produced

missing values

a field left blank

N
narration script

the full spoken text a presenter writes in advance for a recorded or virtual presentation, planned for pacing since the audience cannot give real-time feedback

next step

a proposal to investigate further, short of committing to a final decision

next-step question

a specific, answerable question that turns a visual finding into a direction for further investigation

O
objective-first framework

choosing what you need to learn before choosing which chart will show it

OTIF (On Time In Full)

a supply chain KPI measuring whether a delivery arrived both by the promised date and with the full quantity ordered, combining timing and completeness into one measure

outliers

a number so far outside the expected range that it requires an explanation.

overreach

a claim that assigns a cause, motive, or certainty a chart cannot support on its own

P
parameter

an interactive control that lets a viewer swap which measure or dimension a chart displays

problem statement

a plain description of what is going wrong, before it is sharpened into a specific, answerable business question

Q
quality

the defect or return rate on shipments received, regardless of whether they arrived on time

R
recommendation

a specific next step proposed in response to a finding's implication

reference table

a separate table joined to another dataset to look up additional information, such as a Supplier reference table that carries each supplier's on-time percentage

relationship

a question asking whether two measures move together

renaming

changing a column's label so it's used consistently everywhere it's needed, such as changing "Amt" to "Qty Ordered"

reporting

presenting data or results after the fact, without necessarily connecting them to a specific decision

root cause

the actual underlying reason a pattern or problem exists, as distinct from the pattern itself

S
scatter plot

a chart that plots two measures against each other to show whether they move together

service level

the percentage of shipments a supplier delivers on or before the promised date, tracked supplier by supplier

shelves

the areas across the top and side of a worksheet, such as Columns, Rows, and Marks, where you build encodings by dragging fields onto them

Show Me

a panel of chart-type suggestions based on what is currently on the shelves (same location)

SMART

a goal is SMART when it is specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, and time-bound, so a team knows exactly what success looks like and by when

sorting

reordering rows so a pattern is visible at a glance

static dashboard

a dashboard showing a fixed view to every viewer, whose underlying data may update, but whose displayed parameters the viewer cannot change

Statistical Process Control (SPC) chart

a control chart that plots control limits alongside a trend, used in the Control phase to tell ordinary variation from a real slide back toward the original problem

status visual

a chart built to show whether something currently meets a threshold, rather than to show a trend or a ranked comparison

stockout

a situation where a product is out of stock and an order for it cannot be fulfilled

T
Tableau

a business intelligence and data visualization platform used to build charts, dashboards, and shared analytical views from prepared data

Tableau Prep

a visual, no-code Tableau tool for cleaning, shaping, and automatically refreshing data at a scale beyond what is practical to do by hand in Excel

tooltip

a small box that shows a viewer the exact values behind a chart without cluttering it with every number at once

tradeoff

a risk, cost, or constraint that follows from taking a recommended action, stated alongside the recommendation’s benefit rather than left unstated

trend

a question asking how a measure is changing over time

U
unsupported conclusion

a statement that claims more than the evidence behind it can confirm, whether by inventing a cause, assuming an intent, or reaching a conclusion that the data was never positioned to answer

V
variation

inconsistency in a process's output, such as a supplier's on-time rate swinging month to month

vertical container

a Tableau layout element that stacks worksheets top to bottom

visual emphasis

which visual on a dashboard or in a presentation is shown first, or given the most prominent placement, based on what a specific audience most needs to see

visual management

a real-time dashboard a team checks routinely during the Improve phase to confirm a change is moving performance toward the improved state

visualization

a chart or other visual representation of data used to communicate a pattern or answer a business question

W
waste

in the Lean Six Sigma sense, a cost incurred purely to compensate for a process that isn't performing

wedge selection

using Keep Only or Exclude on a pie chart's slice to filter the view down to, or remove, a single category without rebuilding the chart

workflow

the sequence of stages, problem statement, business question, data, visualization, insight, decision, that carries a question through to a decision

worksheet

in Tableau, one chart
