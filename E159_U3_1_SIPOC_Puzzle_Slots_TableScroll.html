Build the SIPOC: Order Fulfillment

Sort each item into the correct SIPOC category for the Meridian Outfitters Emergency Response Kit order fulfillment process. Use the provided Process steps and fixed examples to identify the high-level process and its boundaries.

Before You Begin

Select an item, then select its matching numbered slot (or drag it directly to a slot). The five Process steps and selected examples are already in place and marked Provided. Sort the remaining items into individual slots that match the SIPOC rows, reading each row across the five columns. If a slot is occupied, the displaced item returns to Items to Sort.

Keyboard and screen reader users: press Enter or Space on an item to select it, then press Enter or Space on a slot to place it. Press Escape to clear your selection.
