Research

Start by identifying words and phrases your audience may use when searching. Consider both the relevance of each keyword and how frequently people search for it.

Analyze

Review your keyword findings for patterns. Look for common topics, emerging trends, and the intent behind searches, such as learning, comparing options, or making a purchase.

Organize

Group related keywords together based on what the audience is trying to accomplish. These groups can help shape content topics, pages, and marketing strategies.
