Scenario

FreshPath delivers healthy meal kits. Its marketing team surveyed customers, reviewed purchase data, and conducted interviews. Use the research below to develop a consumer persona for one of its target customers.

1. Review the Customer Research

Research-based evidence

Survey Data

Most customers are 28–40 years old, work full time, and live in urban or suburban areas.

Purchase Data

Customers frequently select quick-prep meals, family-size portions, and lower-cost weekly plans.

Customer Interviews

Customers want healthier meals but struggle to plan, shop, and cook after work.

Communication Data

Customers respond most often to mobile notifications, email reminders, and short social media videos.
