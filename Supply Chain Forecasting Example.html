How your plan changed

    A sound next step: Do not lock in the 15% cut or an increase based on one signal alone. The older sales pattern supports the forecast, but the competitor closure and new customer inquiries suggest conditions may have changed. Verify the current orders, check the forecast’s assumptions, and then adjust production with the team.

    The goal is to practice revising an operational decision when better evidence arrives. You do not need to use an AI tool or enter any business data to complete this activity.
