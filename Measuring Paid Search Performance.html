Measuring Paid Search Performance

Use the buttons to explore three common paid search metrics.

👁️

Impressions

How many times an ad is shown.

Tells you:
Are people seeing the ad?
🖱️

CPC

How much an advertiser pays for each click.

Tells you:
What does each click cost?
📣

CPM

Cost associated with generating ad impressions.

Tells you:
What does visibility cost?

Key takeaway: Marketers look at these metrics together to understand whether ads are gaining visibility and attracting potential customers efficiently.
